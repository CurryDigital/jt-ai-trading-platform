## Supply Chain Architecture

```
PRIMARY: NVDA (NVIDIA)
├── GPU Design (H100, H200, Blackwell)
└── Revenue: $60B+ (data center)
    ↓ demand surge
    
SECONDARY: Direct Suppliers
├── TSMC (TSM) — Chip fabrication
├── SK Hynix — HBM3 memory
├── Amkor (AMKR) — Packaging
└── Foxconn (2354.TW) — Assembly
    ↓ capacity constraints
    
TERTIARY: Equipment & Materials
├── ASML (ASML) — EUV lithography
├── Applied Materials (AMAT) — Deposition/etch
├── Lam Research (LRCX) — Etch systems
└── Entegris (ENTG) — Materials/purification
    ↓ bottlenecks
    
QUATERNARY: Raw Materials
├── MP Materials (MP) — Rare earths
├── Vertiv (VRT) — Data center cooling
├── Fabrinet (FN) — Optical components
└── Power grid/utilities — Electricity
```
