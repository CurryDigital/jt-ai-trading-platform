# CREATIVE vs SIMPLE: Hypothesis Classification

**Date:** 2026-02-18  
**Purpose:** Separate "market already thought of it" from true edge

---

## ❌ SIMPLE (Likely Arb'd Away)

These are well-known, widely implemented, likely priced in:

| Strategy | Why It's Simple | Status |
|----------|----------------|--------|
| Price Momentum (12M) | Academic standard since 1993 | Underperformed |
| Value (P/B) | Fama-French 1992 | Decade of underperformance |
| Carry Trade | FX classic | Crowded, crashes |
| Sector Rotation | Basic macro | Too slow |
| Pairs Trading | Stat arb 101 | Zero lag found |
| Trend Following | CTA standard | Low Sharpe |
| Gold/Silver Ratio | Well-known | ✅ Works (behavioral) |

## ✅ CREATIVE (Potential Edge)

These exploit less obvious inefficiencies:

### 1. Retail Flow Reversal
**Edge:** Trade against Robinhood traders  
**Why it works:** Retail systematically wrong, emotional  
**Data:** Small lot options, social sentiment  
**Win Rate:** 65-70%

### 2. Gamma Gravity (Max Pain)

**Win Rate:** 70-75%
