## Peer Vulnerability Analysis

### Scoring Methodology: Disruption Vulnerability Score (DVS)

| Dimension | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Human-Labor Moat | 25% | Core product relies on human creativity/expertise |
| AI Replacement Probability | 25% | AI can directly replace core workflow |
| Customer Concentration | 25% | Revenue concentrated in vulnerable segment |
| Response Slowness | 25% | Slow to respond with AI features |

**Scale:** 0-10 for each dimension  
**DVS = Product of all four scores**
