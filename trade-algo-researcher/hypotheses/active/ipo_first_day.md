## Core Idea

IPOs are systematically underpriced. First day pop averages 15-20%. Flip IPOs on opening day for quick gains.

**Academic Research:**
- Loughran & Ritter (2004): IPO underpricing 18% average
- 65-70% of IPOs rise on first day
- Hot IPO markets see 50%+ pops

---

## Signal

**Entry:**
- IPO opens for trading (10:00-11:00)
- Price <+30% from offer (avoid overheated)
- Volume >1M shares

**Exit:**
- Same day before close (15:30)
- Or +10% profit target
