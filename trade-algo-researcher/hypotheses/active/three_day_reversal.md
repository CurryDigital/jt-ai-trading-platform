## Core Idea

**Academic Research:**
- Short-term mean reversion in indices
- "3-day rule" among technicians
- Oversold bounces predictable

**Entry:**
- SPY down 3 days in a row
- Total decline >2%
- Buy on close of 3rd day

**Exit:**

- Or +2% profit
