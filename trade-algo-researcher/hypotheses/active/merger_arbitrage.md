# Hypothesis: Merger Arbitrage (Risk Arbitrage)

**Status:** testing  
**Created:** 2026-02-18  
**Asset Class:** Equities  
**Direction:** Long target  
**Horizon:** 1-6 months  
**Target Win Rate:** 85-90%

---

## Core Idea

When company announces acquisition at premium, target stock trades at discount to deal price due to completion risk. Buy target, capture spread when deal closes.

**Academic Research:**
- Mitchell & Pulvino (2001): Merger arbitrage returns 8-12% annually
- 85-90% of announced deals complete successfully
- Spread compensates for deal failure risk

---

## Signal

**Entry:**
- Announced cash deal
- Target trading at >3% discount to offer price
- Deal expected to close within 6 months

**Exit:**
- Deal completes (capture spread)
- Deal breaks (stop loss)
- Time stop: 6 months

---

Announced cash deals from merger news feeds
