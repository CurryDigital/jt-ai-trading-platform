## Core Idea

Bonds "roll down" the yield curve, capturing price appreciation as maturity shortens. Steep curve = more carry.

**Academic Research:**
- Roll yield is significant component of bond returns
- Steep curve predicts positive roll down
- 70-75% of months positive in normal curve
