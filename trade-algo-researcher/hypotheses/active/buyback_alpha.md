## Core Idea

Companies announcing buybacks see price drift. Buybacks signal undervaluation and provide price support.

**Academic Research:**
- Ikenberry et al. (1995): Market underreaction to buybacks
- 4% abnormal return post-announcement
- Drift persists 6-12 months

---

## Signal

**Entry:**
- Buyback announced
- >5% of shares
- Buy T+1

**Exit:**
- 6 months later
- Or buyback completes
