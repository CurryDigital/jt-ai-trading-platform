### 2. COMBINATION STRATEGY METHODOLOGY
**Key Discovery:** Single signals often fail, but COMBINATIONS create edge

**How to Build Combination Strategies:**
1. **Setup Pattern** (consolidation/accumulation)
   - Inside day, range contraction, doji (weak alone)
2. **Volume Confirmation** (conviction)
   - Volume spike > 2x average
3. **Breakout/Momentum** (direction)
   - Price breakout with threshold (e.g., >2%)

**Formula:** Setup + Volume + Breakout = High Win Rate

**Example Success:**
- Inside Day alone: 48.5% win ❌
- Inside + Volume + Breakout: 74.4% win ✅
