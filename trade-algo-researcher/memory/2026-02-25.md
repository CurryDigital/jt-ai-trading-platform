**Issue:** Tested 8+ hypotheses on same data = false positives expected.

**Math:** At 5% significance, testing 20 hypotheses → 1 false positive expected.

**Mitigation:** Out-of-sample validation + paper trading required.

**Fix:** Always report net returns after costs as primary metric.

#### 5. ⚠️ Survivorship Bias
**Issue:** Universe is current tickers (2025), not historical point-in-time.

**Impact:** Failed companies not included = inflated performance.
