## 🔬 KEY INSIGHTS

### 1. Multi-Timeframe Analysis is Essential
H16 (Multi-TF Trend) achieved Golden status on **6/10 tickers** with exceptional consistency. This validates the approach of requiring alignment across multiple time horizons.

### 2. Time-Based Exit Optimization is Critical
10-day holds significantly outperformed 5-day holds for trend-following strategies. This suggests:
- Let winners run longer
- Patience pays in trending markets
- Exit timing is as important as entry

### 3. Volatility Compression Predicts Breakouts
H13 validated the "coiled spring" hypothesis across multiple assets. Low volatility → high volatility transitions are exploitable.

### 4. Momentum Acceleration > Momentum Level
H14 (RSI Fast/Slow convergence) outperformed static RSI thresholds. It's not just being overbought/oversold—it's the *rate of change* of momentum that matters.

### 5. Short Strategies Fail in Bull Markets
All bearish strategies were rejected or barely validated. This is expected behavior—don't fight the trend. Short research should focus on:
- Inverse ETFs (SQQQ, SH)
- Bear market regime detection
- Put spreads for defined risk
