### ✅ A. Real Backtesting Engine
**Status:** COMPLETE

**What was built:**
- `backtest_engine.py` - Full backtesting system with pandas-based indicators (no TA-Lib required)
- Database table: `gold.backtest_runs`
- 4 strategy types: Mean Reversion, Trend Following, Breakout, Simple

**APIs:**
- `POST /api/strategies/{id}/backtest` - Queue backtest
- `GET /api/strategies/{id}/backtest-history` - View results
- `GET /api/strategies/{id}/backtest-vs-live` - Compare performance
- `GET /api/backtests/all` - All backtests

**How it works:**
1. Fetches historical data from yfinance (or database cache)
2. Runs strategy-specific logic (RSI, Bollinger Bands, EMA crossovers)
3. Simulates trades with commission
4. Calculates metrics: Return, Sharpe, Max Drawdown, Win Rate
5. Stores results in database

### No TA-Lib Required
All indicators calculated using pandas:
- RSI: `pd.Series.rolling().mean()`
- Bollinger Bands: `rolling.mean() ± rolling.std()`
- EMA: `pd.Series.ewm().mean()`
- ATR: Manual calculation with `rolling().max()`
